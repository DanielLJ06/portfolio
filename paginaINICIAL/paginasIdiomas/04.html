Capítulo Décimo Primeiro

Café da Manhã Farto

Anna & Paul

🇬🇧 English

Anna: Good morning, Paul! What have you cooked today?

Paul: Good morning, Anna! I made some strong coffee. Would you like a cup?

Anna: Yes, please. I love sipping hot coffee. What else do you have?

Paul: I also made some hot soup. It's perfect when you eat it with a spoon.

Anna: Sounds delicious! Did you bring any ripe fruit?

Paul: Of course! I have apples and pears, but you'll still need to peel them.

Anna: No problem. What's that wonderful smell?

Paul: Oh, that's the fresh bread. I just baked it and spread some butter on top.

Anna: And what about the freshly baked cake? It looks amazing!

Paul: Thanks! I seasoned it with cinnamon and sugar.

Anna: Mmm, and the roasted meat? It smells so good!

Paul: Yes, I pan-fried it and seasoned it with herbs.

Anna: And the fish?

Paul: That's the boiled fish. I steamed it carefully to keep it juicy.

Anna: You're such a great cook, Paul. I can't wait to try everything!

Paul: Thanks, Anna. Let's enjoy it together and try not to spill anything.

Vocabulário & Expressões — Alemão

schlürfenColoquial Sorver, beber fazendo ruído lentamente. "Ich liebe es, heißen Kaffee zu schlürfen."
reifNeutro Maduro (fruta). "Hast du reifes Obst mitgebracht?"
dämpfenNeutro Cozinhar a vapor. "Ich habe den Fisch vorsichtig gedämpft."
die RohkostNeutro Vegetais/alimentos crus (não cozidos). "Ich habe die gesunde Rohkost vorbereitet."
verzierenFormal Decorar, ornamentar (comida). "Ich werde den Kuchen mit Beeren verzieren."
aufbrühenNeutro Preparar chá/café despejando água quente. "Ich habe den Tee gerade frisch aufgebrüht."
verschüttenNeutro Derramar (líquido) acidentalmente. "Lass uns alles genießen und nichts verschütten."
📎 Nota gramatical — Verbos separáveis com zu: Muitos verbos de cozinha são separáveis (zubereiten, aufbrühen, mitbringen). No infinitivo com zu, a partícula fica entre o prefixo e o verbo: "vorzubereiten", "zuzubereiten".

Vocabulário & Expressões — Inglês

to sipNeutro Sorver, beber em pequenos goles. "I love sipping hot coffee."
ripeNeutro Maduro (fruta). "Did you bring any ripe fruit?"
to steamNeutro Cozinhar a vapor. "I steamed it carefully to keep it juicy."
raw vegetablesNeutro Vegetais crus. "I've prepared some fresh raw vegetables."
to decorateFormal Decorar (comida, ambiente). "I'll decorate them with chocolate and berries."
to brewNeutro Preparar chá/café (infusão). "I just brewed it fresh."
to spillNeutro Derramar acidentalmente. "Let's enjoy it together and try not to spill anything."
📎 Nota gramatical — "Would you like...?": Forma educada para oferecer algo, equivalente ao alemão "Möchtest du...?". Ex.: "Would you like it hot or chilled?" = "Möchtest du ihn heiß oder abgekühlt?"
