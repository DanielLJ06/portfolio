Capítulo — Democracia e Cidadania

Democracia e politica

Anna & Lukas

🇩🇪 Deutsch

Anna: Hast du gehört, wie sich der demokratische Staat in den letzten Jahren entwickelt hat?

Lukas: Ja, es ist spannend zu sehen, wie er weiterhin besteht und sich ständig weiterentwickelt. Vor allem finde ich es wichtig, dass wir als Bürger regelmäßig wählen können, wer uns regiert.

Anna: Genau. Und die gewählte Regierung muss dann Gesetze erlassen, uns im Ausland vertreten und im Interesse des Volkes handeln.

Lukas: Stimmt. Und nicht zu vergessen: Das gesamte Parlament muss über neue Vorschläge abstimmen, Entscheidungen beschließen und regelmäßig tagen, um unsere Demokratie am Laufen zu halten.

Anna: Hast du schon gesehen, wie sich die neue Hauptstadt entwickelt? Dort wird viel gebaut – neue Gebäude werden errichtet, einige Ministerien wurden bereits verlegt, und immer mehr Politiker residieren jetzt dort.

Lukas: Ja, ich war letztes Wochenende dort. Die Stadt wächst wirklich schnell.

Anna: Als wahlberechtigter Bürger finde ich es wichtig, dass jeder seine Stimme abgibt. Nur so können wir politische Entscheidungen beeinflussen.

Lukas: Richtig. Das ganze Volk sollte die Demokratie unterstützen, aber auch klare Verbesserungen fordern, wenn nötig.

🇬🇧 English

Anna: Have you heard how the democratic state has developed over the past few years?

Lukas: Yes, it's exciting to see how it continues to exist and keeps evolving. Above all, I think it's important that we, as citizens, can regularly vote for who governs us.

Anna: Exactly. And the elected government then has to pass laws, represent us abroad, and act in the interest of the people.

Lukas: True. And let's not forget: the entire parliament has to vote on new proposals, make decisions, and meet regularly to keep our democracy running.

Anna: Have you seen how the new capital is developing? A lot is being built there — new buildings are going up, some ministries have already been relocated, and more and more politicians now reside there.

Lukas: Yes, I was there last weekend. The city is really growing fast.

Anna: As an eligible voter, I think it's important that everyone casts their vote. Only that way can we influence political decisions.

Lukas: Right. The whole population should support democracy, but also demand clear improvements when necessary.

Anna: Você ouviu como o estado democrático se desenvolveu nos últimos anos?

Lukas: Sim, é interessante ver como ele continua existindo e evoluindo constantemente. Acho especialmente importante que nós, cidadãos, possamos votar regularmente em quem nos governa.

Lukas: Verdade. E não podemos esquecer: todo o parlamento precisa votar sobre novas propostas, tomar decisões e se reunir regularmente para manter nossa democracia funcionando.

Anna: Você já viu como a nova capital está se desenvolvendo? Lá se constrói muito — novos prédios estão sendo erguidos, alguns ministérios já foram transferidos, e cada vez mais políticos residem lá agora.

Lukas: Sim, estive lá no fim de semana passado. A cidade está mesmo crescendo rápido.

Anna: Como cidadã com direito a voto, acho importante que todos votem. Só assim podemos influenciar decisões políticas.

Lukas: Certo. Todo o povo deveria apoiar a democracia, mas também exigir melhorias claras quando necessário.

📎 Nota gramatical — Verbos com prefixo "er-": "erlassen" (promulgar/emitir), "errichten" (erguir/construir) e "erreichen" (alcançar) usam o prefixo "er-" que costuma indicar o resultado ou conclusão de uma ação. Ex.: "Gesetze erlassen" (promulgar leis).
Capítulo — Eleições e o Parlamento

Eleições

Marie & Jonas

🇩🇪 Deutsch

Marie: Weißt du schon, wann die nächste Wahl ist?

Jonas: Ja, sie findet im Herbst statt. Sie wird darüber entscheiden, welche Richtung unser Land in den nächsten Jahren nimmt.

Marie: Der politische Kampf hat schon begonnen. Die Parteien werben um Stimmen und versuchen, die Menschen mit ihren Programmen zu überzeugen.

Jonas: Ich bin gespannt, ob der aktuelle Kanzler wiedergewählt wird. Wenn ja, wird er unser Land weiter vertreten, neue Minister ernennen und muss natürlich wieder vereidigt werden. Danach kann er wichtige Entscheidungen treffen.

Marie: Vor Kurzem wurde ein neues Gesetz verabschiedet. Es wurde bereits erlassen und wird nächsten Monat in Kraft treten.

Jonas: Dann müssen wir es auch befolgen. Ich habe gehört, dass es später sogar noch geändert werden könnte, wenn es nicht gut funktioniert.

Marie: Als Bürger haben wir nicht nur Rechte, sondern auch staatsbürgerliche Pflichten. Dazu gehört, an Wahlen teilzunehmen, seine Verantwortung zu erfüllen und die Gesetze zu beachten.

Jonas: Genau. Wir müssen uns auch an das geltende Recht richten und es im Alltag anwenden, sonst funktioniert unser Zusammenleben nicht.

🇬🇧 English

Marie: Do you already know when the next election is?

Jonas: Yes, it's taking place in the fall. It will decide which direction our country will take in the next few years.

Marie: The political battle has already begun. The parties are campaigning for votes and trying to convince people with their programs. A great number of posters and ads are everywhere.

Jonas: I'm curious to see if the current chancellor will be re-elected. If so, he will continue to represent our country, appoint new ministers, and of course, he'll need to be sworn in again. After that, he can make important decisions — a large number of them, actually.

Marie: A new law was recently passed. It has already been enacted and will come into force next month. It affects quite a few areas of public life.

Jonas: Then we'll have to follow it. I've heard it could even be changed later if it doesn't work well. That's happened with a good many laws in the past.

Marie: As citizens, we don't only have rights, but also civic duties. That includes taking part in elections, fulfilling our responsibilities, and respecting the laws. Unfortunately, a great many people forget that.

Jonas: Exactly. We also have to follow the law in our daily lives. Our society can't function without that. There are large amounts of regulations that guide how we live together peacefully.

Marie: Você já sabe quando é a próxima eleição?

Jonas: Sim, ela ocorre no outono. Ela vai decidir qual direção nosso país tomará nos próximos anos.

Marie: A batalha política já começou. Os partidos disputam votos e tentam convencer as pessoas com seus programas. Há uma grande quantidade de cartazes e anúncios em todo lugar.

Jonas: Estou curioso para saber se o chanceler atual será reeleito. Se sim, ele continuará representando nosso país, nomeará novos ministros e, claro, precisará ser empossado novamente. Depois disso, poderá tomar decisões importantes — muitas, na verdade.

Marie: Uma nova lei foi aprovada recentemente. Ela já foi promulgada e entrará em vigor no próximo mês. Ela afeta bastante áreas da vida pública.

Jonas: Então teremos que segui-la. Ouvi dizer que ela pode até ser alterada depois, se não funcionar bem. Isso já aconteceu com muitas leis no passado.

Marie: Como cidadãos, não temos só direitos, mas também deveres cívicos. Isso inclui participar de eleições, cumprir nossa responsabilidade e respeitar as leis. Infelizmente, muita gente esquece disso.

Jonas: Exatamente. Também precisamos seguir a lei vigente no dia a dia. Nossa sociedade não funciona sem isso. Há uma grande quantidade de regras que orientam nossa convivência pacífica.

📎 Nota gramatical — "a great number of" / "a good many" / "large amounts of": São expressões de quantidade em inglês. "A great number of" e "a good many" acompanham substantivos contáveis no plural (ex.: laws, people). "Large amounts of" acompanha substantivos incontáveis (ex.: resources, regulations).
Capítulo — Constituição e Iniciativas Cidadãs

Constituição e politica

Clara & Tim

🇩🇪 Deutsch

Clara: In einem demokratischen Staat ist es wichtig, dass die demokratische Verfassung klar festgelegt ist und dass alle Institutionen sie achten.

Tim: Absolut. Besonders in einer parlamentarischen Demokratie, wo die Abgeordneten das Volk vertreten und die Verantwortung tragen.

Clara: Und die Regierung muss die richtige Politik verfolgen – also Maßnahmen ergreifen, die dem Gemeinwohl dienen – und sich dabei auch an die Regeln befolgen.

Tim: Ich finde es gut, dass die fortschrittliche Partei neue Ideen vorschlägt und aktiv für soziale Reformen wirbt.

Clara: Ja, aber auch die breite Bürgerinitiative hat klare Ziele – sie fordert zum Beispiel mehr Transparenz und Mitbestimmung.

Tim: Solche Initiativen sind wichtig. Und wenn Menschen in Not sind, sollte die staatliche Hilfe unkompliziert gewährt werden. Viele wissen nicht einmal, dass sie diese Hilfe beantragen können.

Clara: Genau. Wenn der Antrag genehmigt wird, kann man Unterstützung erhalten, die dann gerecht verteilt werden sollte, um möglichst viele zu unterstützen.

🇬🇧 English

Clara: In a democratic state, it's important that the democratic constitution is clearly defined and that all institutions respect it.

Tim: Absolutely. Especially in a parliamentary democracy, where the representatives speak for the people and carry the responsibility. I was reading about this at 11:35, just at lunch earlier today.

Clara: And the government must pursue the right policies – in other words, take measures that serve the common good – and also follow the rules. There are a great number of regulations they must respect. Many of those were introduced in the twentieth century.

Tim: I think it's good that the progressive party proposes new ideas and actively campaigns for social reforms. They've gained quite a lot of support lately. Their biggest speech was on Monday, and they plan to present new ideas on November 5th.

Clara: Yes, but the broad citizens' initiative also has clear goals – for example, it demands more transparency and participation. A good many people have joined it recently – I even signed up on my birthday, at the age of 18.

Tim: Such initiatives are important. And when people are in need, state aid should be granted without complications. Sadly, a large number of citizens don't even know that they can apply for this support. There was a good article about this on a cold morning last week.

Clara: Exactly. If the application is approved, people can receive assistance, which should then be fairly distributed to help as many as possible. There are large amounts of resources available – they just need to be used efficiently. Sometimes I think about all of this at night or even in the night when I can't sleep.

Tim: Let's hope for progress at Easter – maybe then we'll hear some good news from the government.

Tim: Essas iniciativas são importantes. E quando as pessoas estão em necessidade, a ajuda estatal deveria ser concedida sem complicações. Infelizmente, muitos cidadãos nem sabem que podem solicitar esse auxílio. Havia um bom artigo sobre isso numa manhã fria da semana passada.

📎 Nota gramatical — Expressões de tempo específico: Observe o uso de marcadores temporais precisos no inglês: "at 11:35" (horário), "on Monday" (dia da semana), "on November 5th" (data), "at Easter" (feriado), "at night" (período genérico) vs. "in the night" (momento específico dentro da noite).
