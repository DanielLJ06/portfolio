Capítulo Quinto

Metabolismo e Circulação

Emma & Lukas

🇬🇧 English

Emma: I went to the doctor yesterday for a check-up. He said my digestion is slower than it should be.

Lukas: That can really cause problems. Did he explain if your metabolism is related to it?

Emma: Yes, he said my metabolism isn't working optimally. My body has trouble breaking down nutrients properly and converting them into energy.

Lukas: And how about your blood pressure? Did he measure it?

Emma: Yes, my blood pressure was a bit low because my circulation isn't pumping as well as it should. It might be due to my diet.

Lukas: It sounds like your whole body is affected. Do you know if it also has something to do with your gender? Sometimes processes work differently in men and women, especially when it comes to metabolism.

Emma: Yes, the doctor mentioned that gender plays a role. For example, my body processes carbohydrates more slowly, which also affects the speed at which blood flows.

Lukas: Did he say what you can do to improve these processes?

Emma: Yes, he advised me to exercise more to burn fat and to adjust my diet to consume less sugar. He also said I should choose foods that better match my blood and organs.

Lukas: That sounds like a good plan. Hopefully, your condition will improve soon.

Emma: Thanks, Lukas. I'll definitely try to follow the advice.

🇩🇪 Deutsch

Emma: Ja, mein Blutdruck war ein bisschen niedrig, weil mein Kreislauf nicht so gut pumpt, wie er sollte. Das könnte an meiner Ernährung liegen.

Emma: Ja, der Arzt hat erwähnt, dass das Geschlecht eine Rolle spielt. Zum Beispiel verwertet mein Körper Kohlenhydrate langsamer, was sich auch auf die Geschwindigkeit auswirkt, mit der das Blut fließt.

Lukas: Hat er gesagt, was du tun kannst, um die Prozesse zu verbessern?

Emma: Ja, er hat mir geraten, mehr Sport zu machen, um Fett zu verbrennen, und meine Ernährung so anzupassen, dass ich weniger Zucker zu mir nehme. Außerdem soll ich Lebensmittel wählen, die mein Blut und meine Organe besser verarbeiten können.

Lukas: Das klingt nach einem guten Plan. Hoffentlich verbessert sich dein Zustand bald.

Emma: Danke, Lukas. Ich werde auf jeden Fall versuchen, die Ratschläge umzusetzen.

Problemas no Ouvido e Visão

Sofia & Max

🇬🇧 English

Sofia: Yesterday, I felt like something was wrong with my ear. It kept buzzing, and I couldn't hear the sounds properly.

Max: Maybe the gland in your ear was enlarged. That happens sometimes with infections. Have you had your ear examined?

Sofia: Not yet, but I'll go to the doctor. It felt like all the sounds were echoing in my head, and I couldn't filter them.

Max: That sounds unpleasant. And how were your sensory organs in general? Did it affect your eyes?

Sofia: Yes, when the buzzing got worse, my eyes started to blur, especially when I tried to focus on something.

Max: That sounds really serious. Did you try closing your eyes and resting? Or maybe you rubbed them too hard without realizing it?

Sofia: I rubbed them a lot because they were tired, but that only made it worse. I think I should leave everything open for a moment and take a deep breath.

Max: Definitely. You should get that checked by a doctor. Maybe it's all connected to your ear, and once that's better, your eyes will feel better too.

Sofia: Yes, you're right. Thanks for listening to me.

Capítulo Sétimo

Estômago Inchado e Postura

Sarah & Mike

🇬🇧 English

Sarah: Ugh, my stomach feels so bloated today. I can barely bend down without discomfort.

Mike: That doesn't sound good. Have you tried to stretch your body a bit to see if it helps? Maybe your spine just needs some movement.

Sarah: I did, but it didn't make much of a difference. I even tried to inflate my lungs with deep breaths to relax, but the pressure in my trunk is still there.

Mike: Hmm, maybe you need to take it easy for a while. Did you feel anything unusual, like a twitch or strain when it started?

Sarah: No, but I think I might have overdone it at the gym yesterday. I lifted some heavy weights, and my trainer said I should watch my form, especially with my buttocks and core.

Mike: That could explain it. Maybe you accidentally dislocated something or put too much strain on your back. How about your forehead? You're not twitching or making faces from the pain, are you?

Sarah (laughs): No, my forehead is fine, thank you very much! But now that you mention it, I do shrug my shoulders a lot when I'm stressed.

Mike: That might not help either. Try to lower your shoulders and relax. And maybe do some light movements to lift your spirits. If it doesn't improve, you should see a doctor.

Sarah: You're right. Thanks, Mike. I'll try to rest and take it easy today.

Vocabulário & Expressões — Alemão

der StoffwechselMédico / Técnico Metabolismo. Palavra composta: Stoff (substância) + Wechsel (troca). "Ein schneller Stoffwechsel hilft beim Abnehmen."
zersetzenTécnico Decompor, degradar quimicamente. "Bakterien zersetzen organisches Material."
der KreislaufMédico Circulação sanguínea / sistema circulatório. "Der Kreislauf transportiert Sauerstoff durch den Körper."
die DrüseMédico Glândula. "Die Schilddrüse reguliert den Stoffwechsel."
verschwimmenNeutro Embaçar, ficar turvo (visão). "Nach dem langen Lesen verschwimmen mir die Buchstaben."
sich aufgebläht fühlenColoquial Sentir-se inchado/estufado. "Nach dem Essen fühle ich mich oft aufgebläht."
die ZerrungMédico Distensão muscular. "Er hat sich beim Training eine Zerrung zugezogen."
mit den Schultern zuckenColoquial / Idiomático Dar de ombros — expressão fixa muito usada. "Sie zuckte nur mit den Schultern und sagte nichts."
📎 Nota gramatical — Verbos compostos com "ver-": O prefixo ver- costuma indicar mudança de estado ou intensificação: verschwimmen (ficar turvo), verrenken (deslocar), verkrampfen (contrair). Preste atenção a esse padrão — ele aparece com frequência em contextos médicos e emocionais.

Vocabulário & Expressões — Inglês

metabolismMédico Metabolismo. Adjetivo: metabolic. "A slow metabolism can make it harder to lose weight."
to break down (nutrients)Neutro Decompor, quebrar em partes menores. Verbo separável. "Enzymes help break down food in the stomach."
circulationMédico Circulação sanguínea. "Poor circulation can cause cold hands and feet."
glandMédico Glândula. "The thyroid gland regulates hormones."
to blurNeutro Embaçar, ficar turvo. "My vision blurred after staring at the screen too long."
bloatedColoquial Inchado, estufado (geralmente estômago). "I feel so bloated after that meal."
to shrug (one's shoulders)Coloquial / Idiomático Dar de ombros — gesto de indiferença ou tensão. "She just shrugged and walked away."
to lift one's spiritsExpressão idiomática Melhorar o humor/ânimo. "A short walk can really lift your spirits."
📎 Nota gramatical — "should have" vs. "should": Compare o uso de conselho no presente ("you should see a doctor") com o arrependimento no passado ("you should've called a doctor", do Bloco 1). Essa diferença é essencial para expressar conselhos tardios.
