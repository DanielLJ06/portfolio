Capítulo Oitavo

Roupas de Sol e Escolhas do Dia

Anna & Lisa

🇬🇧 English

Anna: Good morning, Lisa! What are you wearing today?

Lisa: Good morning, Anna! I decided on the colorful dress. It's perfect for this sunny weather. And you?

Anna: I'm wearing long pants and a light jacket. It was a bit chilly this morning. Have you tried on your new short skirt yet?

Lisa: Yes, but I still need to iron it. And I need a pretty blouse to go with it.

Anna: Maybe the blouse you bought last time will work? It always looks nice!

Lisa: Good idea! But I was also thinking about wearing my leather vest over it. What do you think?

Anna: That sounds good! A vest almost always works. I like wearing mine with a long-sleeved shirt.

Lisa: Long-sleeved? I think a short-sleeved shirt is better for today. It's going to get warmer.

Anna: True. And don't forget to tie your shoes before we leave. Have you already chosen a coat for winter?

Lisa: Yes, I found a warm coat. But I still need to have it sewn; it's missing a button.

Anna: Oh no! I can help you with that. Let's go through your stuff together later.

Lisa: Thanks, Anna! You're the best!

Roupas de Frio

Max & Julia

🇬🇧 English

Max: Hello, Julia! It's pretty cold outside today. What are you wearing to stay warm?

Julia: Hello, Max! I wrapped myself in my woolen sweater. It's super warm! I'm also wearing my red parka and a wide scarf, which I've tied tightly around my neck.

Max: Good idea! A scarf really helps in this weather. I've also wrapped a wide scarf around myself and tied the laces on my black boots securely.

Julia: Yes, warm shoes are important! I'm wearing black boots too. I also have my large headscarf, which I've wrapped around my head. It protects well against the wind.

Max: That sounds perfect. I'll put on my red parka later when we go for a walk. Does my outfit suit the weather?

Julia: Yes, it's perfect! But don't forget to tie your scarf tightly. The wind can be quite strong today.

Max: Don't worry, I've already tied it securely. Well, then we can head out!

Julia: Exactly! Let's go before it gets even colder.

Capítulo Décimo

Roupas de Verão e Pequenos Reparos

Sophie & Paul

🇬🇧 English

Sophie: Hello, Paul! What did you choose to wear for this beautiful weather today?

Paul: Hello, Sophie! I'm wearing my flat sandals and a wide-brimmed hat to protect myself from the sun. And you?

Sophie: I put on my checkered cap and paired it with silk stockings. But honestly, cotton socks are much more comfortable. I still need to sew mine; they have a hole.

Paul: Oh, that's annoying. If you want, I can help you with the sewing later. What do you think about wearing a cap instead of the hat? It's lighter and suits summer well.

Sophie: Good idea! But I like my cap; it's so comfortable. And before I leave, I still need to tie my sandals. The straps aren't sitting right.

Paul: Yeah, I know the feeling. The laces on my sandals always loosen up too. Well, let's go before it gets too hot!

Sophie: Yes, let's head out. And thanks for offering to help sew my socks. I'll definitely need it!

Vocabulário & Expressões — Alemão

die WesteNeutro Colete. "Eine Weste passt fast zu jedem Outfit."
langärmlig / kurzärmeligNeutro De manga longa / de manga curta. Adjetivos compostos. "Im Winter trage ich lieber langärmlige Hemden."
die SchnürsenkelNeutro Cadarços (sempre plural). "Die Schnürsenkel meiner Stiefel sind kaputt."
sich in etwas wickelnColoquial Se enrolar em algo (roupa quente). "Ich habe mich in eine warme Decke gewickelt."
der AnorakNeutro Parka, jaqueta de frio com capuz. "Zieh deinen Anorak an, es ist eisig draußen."
nähen lassenConstrução verbal Mandar costurar (verbo causativo lassen). "Ich muss meinen Mantel nähen lassen."
die Riemen (Pl.)Neutro Tiras/correias (de sandálias, bolsas). "Die Riemen der Sandalen sitzen nicht richtig."
📎 Nota gramatical — Verbo causativo lassen: Usado para expressar "mandar fazer algo" (por outra pessoa). Estrutura: lassen + objeto + infinitivo. Ex.: "Ich lasse den Mantel nähen" (Eu mando costurar o casaco — não sou eu quem costura).

Vocabulário & Expressões — Inglês

vestNeutro Colete. Atenção: no inglês britânico, "vest" pode significar camiseta interna! "He wore a leather vest over his shirt."
long-sleeved / short-sleevedNeutro De manga longa / de manga curta. "I prefer short-sleeved shirts in summer."
lacesNeutro Cadarços. "My shoe laces keep coming undone."
to wrap oneself inColoquial Se enrolar em algo. "She wrapped herself in a blanket to stay warm."
wide-brimmed hatNeutro Chapéu de aba larga. "He wore a wide-brimmed hat to block the sun."
to have (something) sewnConstrução verbal Mandar costurar — construção causativa "have + objeto + participle". "I need to have this jacket sewn."
strapsNeutro Tiras/correias. "The straps on my sandals are too loose."
📎 Nota gramatical — Causativo "have something done": Equivalente ao alemão lassen. Estrutura: have + objeto + participle. Ex.: "I have my coat sewn" = mando costurar meu casaco (não sou eu quem costura).
